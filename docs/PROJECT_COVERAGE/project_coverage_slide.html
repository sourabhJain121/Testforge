Suitable Agentic Design Patterns
Implement suitable and relevant agentic design patterns. Evaluate them against other agentic patterns.
 Multi-agent cognitive decomposition (testforge/orchestrator.py): TargetManager (ingestion), Generator (synthesis), Evaluator (sandbox execution), and Critic (adversarial reflection). Implements multi-turn reflection loop (1-3 iterations) guided by surviving mutants. Evaluated against linear single-prompt generation: Baseline achieved 5.26% on Discount and 3.10% on Logistics; agentic reflection achieved 94.44% and 75.21% adjusted mutation scores. Verified in tests/test_agentic_pipeline.py (16). COMPLETE
