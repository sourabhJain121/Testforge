## 4. Verification of Provenance Gating Logic

The TestForge orchestrator enforces provenance through typed contracts in `testforge/blackboard.py`:
- `SuiteSpace.current_test_code` is protected by `BlackboardLock`.
- When tests are validated, `SuiteSpace.validated_suite_hash` is recorded via `hashlib.sha256(test_code.encode("utf-8")).hexdigest()`.
- Prior to running mutation testing, the orchestrator asserts that the hash of the suite being submitted to the sandbox matches `blackboard.get_suite_space().validated_suite_hash`.
- Any modification during critique or reflection produces a new candidate with an updated hash, which must re-pass validation before being accepted.
